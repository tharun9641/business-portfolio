| D-06 | 2026-10-18 | Water reflections of the bridge are **rendered live** (mirrored, blurred, low-opacity copy of the towers and bascule layers, with a light displacement shimmer) instead of baked into `water.avif`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | A baked reflection would show a closed bridge while the real bridge opens. | D-07 | 2026-10-18 | **Every ambient loop is procedural**: mist drift is tiled PNG bands translated with GSAP; water shimmer is an SVG `feTurbulence` displacement on a small, isolated layer, or a WebGL shader where the 3D canvas already exists; capsule twinkle is shader uniforms; particles are a lightweight canvas. **No video files.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | GPU-cheap, loops seamlessly, zero network weight, scales with device tier. Generated video isn't available on the current tools anyway.                                                                                                       | proposed                                                   |
| D-08 | 2026-10-18 | Mist bands are **generated procedurally** (fractal noise turned into alpha, horizontally tileable) with a sharp-based script, not with AI.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | AI mist won't tile seamlessly, and the drift loop needs a perfect tile. Deterministic and tiny.                                                                                                                                               | proposed                                                   |
| D-09 | 2026-10-18 | Alpha cut-outs: polygon masks for the bascules (exact pivots), flux.1.1-pro props on a flat backdrop, then matted, and an **offline BiRefNet (MIT) matting script** via `@huggingface/transformers` for organic shapes. The script is a devDependency and never ships.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | polli has no background-removal endpoint, and Flux has no transparency option.
